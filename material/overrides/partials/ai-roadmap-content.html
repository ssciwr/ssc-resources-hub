External benchmarks and design rationale

Reviewed selected curricula on 24 November 2026. These are design references, not evidence that every SSC prerequisite is mandated by the providers.

  • roadmap.sh — AI Engineer: applied engineering with existing models; distinguish learning branches from strict requirements.
  • IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate: distinguish conventional ML/DL and generative AI; SSC excludes training and extended mathematical theory.
  • DeepLearning.AI — Building Systems with the ChatGPT API: programmatic calls before multi-step workflows, with output checks.
  • DeepLearning.AI — Agentic AI: implement tool use and workflows, with evaluation throughout.
  • DeepLearning.AI — RAG: retrieval, embeddings, generation and evaluation form a coherent topic; neither MCP nor agents is required.
  • DeepLearning.AI — MCP: standardized interfaces to tools and data, not a prerequisite for every AI application.

SSC design decisions: build independent focused competencies, integrate only where technically necessary, and require an implemented application for application evaluation. Full external competency extraction, slide reuse verification and exercise timing remain pending.

Scope and validation notes

v0.13 — dependency review. 29 course options (13 MVP options, 12 distinct MVP competencies). A unified diagram shows all learning paths, with explicit OR gates. A04 follows A03 as a recommendation, not a requirement; a guided existing client suffices. A07 accepts any one of A02, A05 or A06 (or equivalent). M03 uses A01's programmatic model interaction; M03 is additionally required for the LLM-serving variant of M04. Standard prerequisites and alternatives are competency requirements, not mandatory course attendance.

Coverage labels describe material identified during the first assessment, not readiness to teach. Six PDF decks and the public Agentic TDD course reference inform the reuse decisions. SSC-07 slides and the detailed SSC-01 slide/exercise review remain pending. The bibliography includes supplied slide references as source records, not as public downloads.

External prerequisites are hidden from the graph and listed in course details. Scope is practical use of existing traditional ML, deep learning and generative AI models, not training. Comprehensive external benchmarking, exercise testing, dataset licensing, four-hour feasibility and reference stack validation remain pending. Public materials are intended for GitHub; dedicated tutor materials remain internal.
